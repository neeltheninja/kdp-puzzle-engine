Connect Chrome

Open Chrome with remote debugging on port 9222 and a dedicated profile. Sign in to KDP in that browser. Chrome must run on the same computer as the engine.

chrome --remote-debugging-port=9222 --user-data-dir=./kdp-chrome

Use the path to your Chrome executable on Windows or macOS.

Listing tools use your existing browser session. KDP page changes may require updates to the automation.

IN THE WORKSHOP

Activity

Make your first book.

  1. Choose word search, Sudoku, or mazes.
  2. Pick a theme, add your title, and choose the puzzle count.
  3. Preview a puzzle or its solution, then create your book.
  4. Open your library to review the PDFs, run preflight, and download everything.

The engine runs on your computer. Keep its terminal open while working. To use another device on your network, start with python studio.py --host 0.0.0.0 and open the access link using your computer’s LAN address.

Cover concepts are approximate. The generated cover and PDF files in your library are the final artwork. Preflight checks the engine’s existing rules; it does not guarantee KDP acceptance.
